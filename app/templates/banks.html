Connect Kasboek to Enable Banking

Kasboek reads your bank accounts through Enable Banking (read-only, never payments). You need your own free application there, once:

  1. Sign up at enablebanking.com and open the control panel.
  2. Create an application. As redirect URL, enter the address of this Kasboek followed by /banks/callback (it must start with https://).
  3. Enable Banking gives you a .pem file: the application's private key. Upload it here.
